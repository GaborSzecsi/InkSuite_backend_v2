- Reserve and minimum-payout amounts are frozen by the royalty engine; the payments module does not recalculate royalty rates.
- Decimal split arithmetic with a deterministic cent remainder, no assumed agency percentage.
- Secure banking uses tenant slug TEXT while the public ledger uses tenant UUID; both are validated explicitly.

- Immutable allocation/source/instruction snapshots and append-only events, approvals, balance snapshots and shadow reconciliation records.

- Pending/submitted transfers reserve capacity; only simulation settlement reduces simulation eligibility. Returns append compensating shadow postings.
